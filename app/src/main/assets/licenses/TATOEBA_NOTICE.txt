app/src/main/assets/lessons.json and docs/例句来源索引.tsv. Entries using retained
English with a newly authored Chinese translation are explicitly marked as such.

Tatoeba sentence attribution is not applied to the replacement text.

New Chinese translations, example usage explanations, and original examples were
AI-assisted and individually reviewed in preparing this project. These sentences
are not excerpts from the user's PDF or official CET-6 examination questions.
